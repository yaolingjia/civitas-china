Services · 服务范围

Early design focus—not construction documents

We concentrate on front-end design quality and speed. Construction document production is outside our scope.

SE

Scheme exploration

Massing, options studies, and design alternatives that keep Principals in the driver’s seat while expanding option throughput.

DD

Design development

Deepening design intent with architect-led coordination—drawings, models, and packages that move cleanly toward later phases.

RF
